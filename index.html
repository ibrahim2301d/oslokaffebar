A Place for Coffee

We believe in the quiet craft of coffee making. No rush, just carefully sourced beans, precise extraction, and an atmosphere that invites you to pause.

Drawing inspiration from the bright, minimalist aesthetics of Nordic cafes, Oslo Kaffebar serves as a serene escape from the Berlin energy outside our doors.

Our Story

Established in 2012, Oslo Kaffebar was born from a simple desire: to bring the clarity and warmth of Scandinavian coffee culture to the busy streets of Berlin-Mitte.

The Philosophy

We are an independent space. We believe that a cafe should be an extension of the neighborhood—a place where quality is uncompromising, but the atmosphere is completely unpretentious.

Our approach to coffee focuses on light roasts that highlight the natural characteristics of the bean. We favor clarity, sweetness, and balance in every cup.

The Space

The interior of Oslo Kaffebar was designed to reflect our Nordic inspirations. Natural wood, raw textures, and ample daylight create a warm environment.

Whether you are here for a quick morning espresso at the bar or spending hours reading accompanied by vinyl records playing in the background, the space adapts to your rhythm.
